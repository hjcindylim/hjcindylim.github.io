Design critique: Generative AI for Everyone

An instructional design review of Week 1 of a popular introductory AI course, judged on instructional value, materials, ease of use and motivational design.

Course reviewed
Generative AI for Everyone by DeepLearning.AI, delivered on Coursera. Week 1: Introduction to Generative AI.
Course audience
A general audience with little or no technical background in AI
My role
Sole author, about 1,500 words
Context
Course project, EDUC 5030 Designing Effective Learning Environments, Iowa State University
Year
2025

Key findings

  • Well aligned for its audience. The objectives, sequencing and quizzes all aim at conceptual understanding, which suits an entry-level course for learners with uneven prior knowledge.
  • Consistent language lowers cognitive load. Quiz items reuse the wording of the lectures, so learners can put their attention on the concepts.
  • Some examples age faster than the concepts. Quiz items about knowledge cutoffs and missing web access may not match what learners see in newer AI tools, which can make the course look outdated.
  • Learner agency is underdeveloped. Tool suggestions stay implicit, with no explicit prompts to experiment or reflect.

Recommendations

  • Separate lasting concepts from time-bound examples with short annotations or update notes.
  • Add optional prompts for hands-on experimentation and reflection, keeping the course's approachable tone.
