About

Instructional designer and educator

I've designed and taught face-to-face, online and blended courses for 16+ years, mostly in higher education. I'm completing a Ph.D. in Applied Linguistics and Technology with a co-major in Human-Computer Interaction at Iowa State University, where I also earned a Graduate Certificate in Instructional Design.

How I work

I design for people who are trying something new. I want students and faculty to experiment with technology confidently, with clear guardrails and a concrete finish line.

I adopt a new tool when it improves the learning experience, and I show its limits as openly as its strengths. I test materials with real users and revise from what I see.
